**Linguistic Differences Between Phishing and Legitimate Emails** — Independent Corpus-Based NLP Research (2026)

Conducted a feature-based comparative analysis of 1,148 phishing and legitimate emails across 19 linguistic and stylistic indicators, applying Welch's t-tests, Holm correction, and Cohen's d. The term paper reported significant group differences in 16 features, particularly second-person reference, urgency and readability. A separate GitHub demonstration documents feature extraction and statistical workflows; original email-level data and scripts are not available for independent replication.
